Maple Survivors

Import your MapleStory v83 data

Checking your saved data...

Maple Survivors draws every sprite, map and sound from your own copy of MapleStory v83 (GMS). Nothing is bundled with the game. The importer reads your files once, in this page, and keeps the result in this browser's storage. Your files are not uploaded anywhere.

Choose the folder of your v83 installation: the one with Map.wz, Mob.wz, Character.wz and the other .wz files.

Choose a .zip of that folder, or drop it on this window. It does not need to be unpacked.

Enter the address of the folder holding the .wz files (for example https://host/folder/, so that https://host/folder/Mob.wz exists), or of one .zip file.

The files come straight from the host you name, to this browser. Nothing goes through us, and the host has to allow it (CORS).
